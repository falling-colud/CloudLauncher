This is a share link for CloudLauncher, a Minecraft launcher for Windows. It opens in the launcher rather than in the browser, because you accept it with your CloudLauncher account.

Your link

Paste the link you were sent into the launcher, as below.

  1. Install CloudLauncher and sign in, or make an account. Download for Windows
  2. Open Sharing in the sidebar and press Redeem a link.
  3. Paste the link. The launcher shows who is sharing what, and what it lets you do, before you press Accept.
  4. Play. A shared instance lands in your instances: open it and press Download to fetch its files.

An invitation sent to you by name only works for that account. A link can expire or be taken back; if yours doesn't work, ask whoever sent it for a new one.
